# Community standards

We want Music Player discussions and contributions to be useful and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about code and behavior, not personal attacks.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct their impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's paths, logs, credentials, or other private data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through GitHub; do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication.
