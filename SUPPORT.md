# Support

For a reproducible bug, open a [bug report](https://github.com/kalabhaftu/music-player/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). For a usage question, check the [README](README.md) and [release guide](docs/release.md) first.

When asking for help, include:

- Music Player version and whether it is a portable or installed build.
- Windows version and device architecture (x64 or ARM64).
- The shortest steps that reproduce the behavior, plus what you expected and what happened.
- Whether the problem repeats after restarting the app.

Logs are available at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MusicPlayer\Logs` and from Settings. Review them before sharing: they may contain usernames, local file paths, and media metadata. Attach only the relevant excerpt and redact private details. Do not upload music files or database backups unless a maintainer has arranged a private, appropriate way to receive them.

Music Player is a local library and playback app. Support requests do not require sharing your music collection.
